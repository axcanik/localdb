Setup Guidelines

  1. Create Discord Bot: Go to the Discord Developer Portal, click New Application, and set up a Bot.
  2. Enable Gateway Privileged Intents:
    Under the Bot tab of your application, scroll down and toggle on:
    • Presence Intent
    • Server Members Intent
    • Message Content Intent (Required)
  3. Invite Bot with Admin Role: Generate an invitation URL under OAuth2 -> URL Generator. Select bot and applications.commands scopes, grant Administrator permissions, and authorize it in your guild.
  4. Developer Mode: Open Discord settings, go to **Advanced**, and toggle on **Developer Mode** to easily copy Server/Channel IDs.
  5. Slash Commands: After deploying, type / in the channel. The bot will automatically register the commands:
    • /add_uid [uid] [note] [days]
    • /remove_uid [uid]
